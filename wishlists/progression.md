# Progression wishlist

How players grow with Claudenspiel. The rule: progress widens what you can do and how you look; it never makes anyone stronger at a table of equals. ✅ done, ▶ next up, ○ later.

## Learning
- ▶ Three archetypes to start (Archbishop, Hedonist, Gardener); the other three unlock by playing, each with a short guided game.
- ✅ 1.0-pre Archetype mastery: ten levels per archetype (marks: 1 a game, 2 a win, 2 for the archetype's goal), four titles each, three strategy notes opening at levels 2, 5 and 8. Goals set near the top third of tactician games.
- ○ Mastery cosmetics: a meeple style or card back per archetype at level 10.

## A campaign against bots: The Long Road to the City
- ✅ 1.0-pre Act I (6 stops), the prairie: against greedy bots, one rule switched on at each stop (build and claim, banking, burying, two claims and Ascension, war, contest), a different archetype at each, a pixel map of the road. Difficulty measured with bots: 62% → 27% (par 33% at the 3-seat finale).
- ✅ 1.0-pre Act II, the river towns: the full game against tactician and warlord bots, each town with a custom (5 meeples, the market, Burn, no Sword cards, hand 5 and draw 3, a four-seat finale with market and Burn), a moonlit river map. Measured with a tactician as the player: 48% → 25–32%.
- ○ Act II twist still unused: start with a Ruined card on the board.
- ✅ 1.0-pre Act III, the city at dawn: six quarters held by named Genius champions with pixel portraits, customs from the rules on trial (guest-right, hallowed ground, the audit, Lent, the tourney), a three-Genius finale; difficulty set by a ◇ gift per stop, measured 23–38%.
- ✅ 1.0-pre Stars per stop: win; win by the stop's par round (the median winning round); a challenge in the spirit of the lesson. The engine counts each player's lost cards.

## Collection
- ✅ 1.0-pre The Pilgrim's Folio: twelve cards (two a suit) about the road, opened by winning Act I, then a New game option (on by default), in the sandbox and in the Library under its own heading. A pixel waymark marks folio cards.
- ○ A River folio (opened by Act II) and a City folio (Act III). Candidates: a ferry that moves a card across a line, a tollhouse that charges rivals for building in its line, guild cards that share ◇ between their claimants.

## Recognition
- ✅ 0.3 The Book of Deeds: 19 achievements to start (Pyrrhic: win with your Foundation one enemy from Overwhelm; Five Suits: make a stack Ascend; Patience: win after taking back a meeple; Grave Robber: bury 10 cards in one game).
- ✅ 1.0-pre Own Counsel (deed 21): beat a Genius without once asking it for counsel.
- ○ Counsel that fades over the campaign: free in Act I, rationed in Act II, gone in Act III.
- ○ Cosmetics: card backs, meeple styles (pewter, gold leaf, bone), board backdrops (dawn, the river ford, the city gate), a heraldic shield built from mastered archetypes.
- ○ Painted cards: paint a card in Dithry once you've mastered it, and show your version to the table.

## Competition (once Claudenspiel goes online)
- ○ Ratings per player count, shown as heraldic ranks (Squire, Knight, Baron, Count, Duke).
- ○ Eight-week seasons, each with one featured rule variant.
- ○ Beat the Genius: a weekly fixed deal that everyone plays against the Genius, with a leaderboard.
