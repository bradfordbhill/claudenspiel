# Progression wishlist

How players grow with Claudenspiel. The rule: progress widens what you can do and how you look; it never makes anyone stronger at a table of equals. ✅ done, ▶ next up, ○ later.

## Learning
- ▶ Three archetypes to start (Archbishop, Hedonist, Gardener); the other three unlock by playing, each with a short guided game.
- ✅ 1.0-pre Archetype mastery: ten levels per archetype (marks: 1 a game, 2 a win, 2 for the archetype's goal), four titles each, three strategy notes opening at levels 2, 5 and 8. Goals set near the top third of tactician games.
- ○ Mastery cosmetics: a meeple style or card back per archetype at level 10.

## A campaign against bots: The Long Road to the City
- ▶ Act I (6 stops), the prairie: against greedy bots, one rule taught at each stop.
- ○ Act II, the river towns: tactician and warlord bots, with rule twists ("no Sword cards in the deck", "start with a Ruined card").
- ○ Act III, the city: the Genius, as named rivals with fixed archetypes (The Abbess, a Genius Archbishop).
- ○ Stars per stop (win; win within 6 rounds; win without losing a card).

## Collection
- ○ Folios: themed packs of 12–15 new cards (the Grounds folio, a River folio) unlocked by the campaign, mixed into solo games and offered as table options.

## Recognition
- ✅ 0.3 The Book of Deeds: 19 achievements to start (Pyrrhic: win with your Foundation one enemy from Overwhelm; Five Suits: make a stack Ascend; Patience: win after taking back a meeple; Grave Robber: bury 10 cards in one game).
- ○ Cosmetics: card backs, meeple styles (pewter, gold leaf, bone), board backdrops (dawn, the river ford, the city gate), a heraldic shield built from mastered archetypes.
- ○ Painted cards: paint a card in Dithry once you've mastered it, and show your version to the table.

## Competition (once Claudenspiel goes online)
- ○ Ratings per player count, shown as heraldic ranks (Squire, Knight, Baron, Count, Duke).
- ○ Eight-week seasons, each with one featured rule variant.
- ○ Beat the Genius: a weekly fixed deal that everyone plays against the Genius, with a leaderboard.
